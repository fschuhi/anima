`pdf-annotations` started strong, but went quickly into hibernation, mainly because it involved software running in parallel on Windows 11 under Parallels and macOS. The reason to choose such a tricky setup was motivated by my love for PDF-Xchange-Viewer: no frills, keyboard driven ... but not supported anymore. And then I realized that Internet Archive PDFs generated from books are layered in a way that messes with PDF-Xchange-Viewer annotation capabilities.
